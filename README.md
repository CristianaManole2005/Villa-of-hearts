# Villa of hearts: Make Them Yours

## Game Story

### Welcome to Villa of Hearts!

You are the newest male contestant to arrive at a luxurious tropical villa, where four female contestants have already started forming friendships, relationships, and rivalries.

However, arriving late comes with a disadvantage. The girls have already settled into life at the villa, and you must prove that you deserve a place among them.

Your ultimate goal is to **win the hearts of the four contestants, build meaningful relationships, and secure your place in the Final Recoupling Ceremony.**

To achieve this, you must explore the villa, interact with the girls, and complete unique challenges assigned by each of them.

Every contestant has a different personality, and her challenge reflects her interests.

Successfully completing a challenge rewards you with a **special heart** and increases your relationship score with that specific contestant.

But collecting hearts is only part of the journey. Your interactions and decisions will determine how the girls feel about you.

Some contestants may become close friends, while others may develop stronger romantic connections with you.

As you progress, you will unlock new areas of the villa, discover secrets, and face increasingly difficult challenges.

At the end of the game, you must participate in the **Final Recoupling Ceremony**, where your relationships and decisions determine your future on the island.

Will you win someone's heart, find your perfect match, or leave the villa alone?

**Make them yours!** ❤️
